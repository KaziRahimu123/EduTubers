# ⚖️ EduTubers — 90-Second Judge's Guide

Welcome IBM Challenge Judges! Follow these 5 quick steps to test EduTubers on the live app in under 90 seconds.

---

### Step 1: Open the Live Application
Visit [https://edutubers.com](https://edutubers.com) and click **"Start for Free"** to log in via Auth0.

### Step 2: Upload Content (Multi-Source Ingestion)
Go to `/generate` and upload a PDF document, video link, or audio file (up to 3 files / 100 MB).

### Step 3: Experience 5 Active-Recall Formats
Watch the hybrid AI engine build 5 distinct formats in under 45 seconds:
- 📚 **Flashcard Decks**
- ✅ **Audience Quizzes**
- 🧩 **Interactive Challenges**
- 📄 **Content Guides**
- ⭐ **Illustrated Explainers**

### Step 4: Test the Built-in Studio Editor
Click into any generated format to manually edit questions, rewrite terms, or add custom cards before publishing.

### Step 5: Test Audience Analytics & AI Action Plan
Take a quiz attempt on a published link, then open `/analytics/[id]` to view:
- **Questions Ranked (Most → Least Wrong)**
- Click **`Generate Insights`** to watch **IBM Granite 3.0** write a targeted **60-Second Video Recap Script**!
